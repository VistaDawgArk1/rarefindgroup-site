Bottle Chase: find what you’re chasing

Allocated bourbon and whiskey near you, reported by hunters who just saw it on the shelf.

Coming soon for iPhone

What it does

  • Live sightings mapRecent finds near you, with bottle counts, shelf prices and photos.
  • Watch list alertsGet a notification when a bottle you’re hunting turns up in your range.
  • Trusted reportsOther hunters confirm “Still there” or “All gone”, with at-store and photo badges.
  • Drops and eventsA calendar of store releases, raffles, tastings and barrel pick parties.
  • Hunting partiesPrivate groups to share finds and chat with friends.
  • Paste a postSaw a find on Facebook or in a group text? Paste it and the report fills itself in.

Where it works

Thousands of liquor stores and distilleries across Alabama, Arkansas, Florida, Georgia, Indiana, Kentucky, Missouri, Mississippi, Tennessee and Texas, with more states on the way. Missing a store? Add it in the app.

Free, and private by design

No ad networks, no tracking and no sign-up form: the app makes an account for you. Stores that pay to be featured are always labeled “Sponsored.” Your exact location stays on your phone. Read the privacy policy.

Bottle Chase is for adults 21 and over. Please drink responsibly, and respect each store’s rules and staff.
